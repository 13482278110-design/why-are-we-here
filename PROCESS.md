# Process

I first gave the assignment brief to ChatGPT and asked it to analyze the requirements. I also asked ChatGPT to generate some possible answers to the main question: why should I learn programming when AI can increasingly do it for me?

I rejected these early answers because they were too general and abstract. They discussed broad ideas about AI, programming, and creativity, but they did not reflect my own experience or what I actually thought about the question.

Instead, I explained my own experiences and thoughts to ChatGPT in Chinese, including my traditional art and design background, my experience of relying on AI in this course, and my thoughts about learning programming. I then used ChatGPT to help organize these ideas, improve the flow of my writing, and translate them into English.

I also used ChatGPT to search for relevant sources about AI-assisted programming, programming education, and creative coding. I checked that the sources existed and were relevant before including them in my references.

Finally, I used ChatGPT to make the English more concise and fluent without changing my main ideas. After finishing the essay, I also asked ChatGPT to guide me through the final checks and the Git commands needed to submit my work correctly.
